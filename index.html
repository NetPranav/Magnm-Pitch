What an Urban-style platform does

Three kinds of people use it. Each gets their own screens.

Customer
  1. Browse services and prices
  2. Pick a date and a time slot
  3. Pay online or in cash
  4. Track the job, then rate it
Service provider
  1. Get a job request
  2. Accept or reject it
  3. Mark the job done
  4. Get paid after your commission
You, the owner
  1. Add services and set prices
  2. See every booking
  3. Manage your providers
  4. Track earnings and fees
